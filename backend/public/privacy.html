Privacy Policy

Last updated: 4 October 2026

In short: we keep your account details and usage records on our server, send your prompts and code to an AI model provider so it can answer, remove personal data and secrets from them first, never sell your data, and never use your code to train models. You can download or delete your data at any time from your dashboard.

Who we are

VynorAI (vynor.lk) is an AI coding assistant operated from Sri Lanka. For this policy we are the controller of your personal data under the Personal Data Protection Act, No. 9 of 2022. Contact: prasadexperior@gmail.com.

Where your data goes

  • Our server (Leaseweb, United States) stores your account, usage records, cache and memory.
  • DeepSeek (China) receives your prompts and code to generate answers. Before anything is sent, we remove secrets (API keys, passwords, private keys, tokens) and personal data (email addresses, phone numbers, NIC numbers, card numbers), and we replace your identity with an anonymous code that changes daily. DeepSeek never receives your name, email or payment details.
  • Cloudflare (global network) carries all traffic to vynor.lk securely and runs the sign-up bot check.
  • PayHere (Sri Lanka) processes payments.

These transfers outside Sri Lanka are necessary to provide the service. By creating an account you agree to them. We do not sell or rent your personal data, and we do not use your code to train or fine-tune any model.

Your rights

Under the Personal Data Protection Act you can:

  • Access and download your data: Dashboard → Account → Download my data.
  • Delete your account and data: Dashboard → Account → Delete account. This takes effect immediately; backups expire within 14 days.
  • Correct your details, withdraw consent or object to processing by emailing us. Withdrawing consent means we can no longer provide the service.
  • Complain to the Data Protection Authority of Sri Lanka (dpa.gov.lk).

Security

Passwords are hashed with bcrypt, API keys are encrypted, traffic is encrypted end to end, and access to administration is protected by two separate factors. If a breach affects your personal data, we will notify you and the Data Protection Authority as the law requires.

Changes

If we change this policy in a way that matters, we will tell you by email or in your dashboard before the change applies.
